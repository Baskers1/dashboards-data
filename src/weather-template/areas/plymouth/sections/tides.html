About this data

High (H) and low (L) water at Devonport, Plymouth, height in metres, times already adjusted for BST. Plymstock and Saltash use the same table; Totnes has its own (Totnes tide times). Sunrise and sunset shown for planning daylight. Tables: tidetimes.org.uk (derived in part from UK Hydrographic Office data; personal use). The curve is a smooth estimate drawn between the listed high and low waters, and shaded areas are night. Weather and wind can shift tide times and heights a little.
