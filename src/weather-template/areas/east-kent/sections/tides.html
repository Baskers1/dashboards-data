About this data

High (H) and low (L) water for the selected town (Ramsgate, Margate, Broadstairs and Deal each have their own table; Sandwich shows high water only, as Dover plus 40 minutes), height in metres, times already adjusted for BST. Sunrise and sunset shown for planning daylight. Tables: tidetimes.org.uk (derived in part from UK Hydrographic Office data; personal use). The curve is a smooth estimate drawn between the listed high and low waters, and shaded areas are night. Weather and wind can shift tide times and heights a little.
